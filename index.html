Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SecureVault handles information when you use the SecureVault Windows desktop application.

1. Overview

SecureVault is designed as a local-first desktop application. The demo version stores your vault and encrypted files on your Windows device. It does not require you to create a cloud account, and it does not provide cloud synchronization.

2. Information you store in SecureVault

Depending on the features you use, you may enter or import information such as saved login details, notes, passwords, and files. This content is stored locally in the SecureVault application data folder, normally:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\SecureVault

The application uses encryption for vault records and encrypted file content. Key-related metadata is also stored locally. Your master password and recovery code are sensitive: anyone who obtains the recovery code may be able to regain access to the vault.

3. Information collection and sharing

The current demo is not designed to send the contents of your vault or your encrypted files to the developer. It does not provide cloud backup or synchronization. The demo's sponsor/advertisement panels are static in-app content; they are not connected to an advertising network.

Your use of Windows, your internet provider, security software, or other third-party services may be subject to their own privacy practices. This policy does not cover those services.

4. Where information is stored and how long it is kept

Application data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall/remove it yourself. Uninstalling the application may not remove the separate SecureVault data folder. If you want to remove your vault, first make sure you understand that deleting its files can permanently destroy your stored data.

You are responsible for protecting your Windows account and any backups you create. Anyone with access to your Windows user account or your recovery code may be able to access your information.

5. Security

SecureVault uses cryptographic protection for locally stored vault data and files. No software or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. This is an educational/demo application and has not been represented as an independently audited password manager. Keep your application updated, use a strong master password, store your recovery code securely offline, and maintain a protected backup.

6. Children’s privacy

SecureVault is not designed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the demo application.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
